Frequently Asked Questions

Our system uses machine learning to analyze patterns in historical insurance claim data. It evaluates factors like driver characteristics, vehicle information, and regional statistics to predict the likelihood of a future claim.

We need information about the driver (age, driving history), the vehicle (type, age), and the region where the vehicle is primarily driven. All data is anonymized and processed securely.

Our model achieves over 90% accuracy on test data. We continuously monitor and improve the model as we receive more claims data.

This is currently a demonstration project. For commercial use, please contact our team to discuss licensing options.
