About Insurance Risk Analyzer

Our mission is to revolutionize insurance risk assessment using advanced machine learning techniques.

Project Overview

This tool uses machine learning to estimate the probability of an auto insurance claim based on driver characteristics, vehicle information, and regional factors. Our model achieves over 90% accuracy in predicting claim likelihood.

The system helps insurance companies set fair premiums while enabling them to identify high-risk customers who may require additional verification.

Technology Stack

  • Python & Flask (Backend)
  • Bootstrap 5 (Frontend)
  • Scikit-Learn (Machine Learning)
  • Pandas & NumPy (Data Processing)
  • Matplotlib & Seaborn (Visualization)
